Privacy Policy

Dodgeline · Last updated November 7, 2026

Dodgeline is a game made by Petra Jakljevič. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

In short: Dodgeline does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your best score, your lives and refill timer, your selected theme and your sound and haptics settings are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores are posted to Dodgeline's leaderboard so you can compare them with friends and players worldwide. This is handled entirely by Apple under its own privacy policy; Dodgeline's developer does not receive or store your Game Center details. You can stop this at any time by signing out of Game Center in the Settings app.

In-app purchases

Dodgeline Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and the developer never sees your payment details. Apple's privacy policy covers that transaction: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Dodgeline does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
