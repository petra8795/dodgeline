How to play

  • Tap anywhere to dash to the other wall.
  • Dash away from a block at the last moment for a close call. Close calls raise your multiplier up to ×5.
  • Close calls also fill the walls. When they're full, Overdrive lets you smash through blocks for a few seconds.
  • Every 20 blocks you enter a new zone: sliders, then lasers (never dash through one), then mayhem.
  • Changed your mind? Double-tap to bounce straight back to the wall you left.
  • Grab a glowing ? box for a surprise: a shield, slow-mo, 2× points, a tiny block, turbo, a trippy screen or fog. It lasts a few seconds.
  • The longer you survive, the more the world around you changes.

Lives

You have up to 5 lives. Each run uses one, and one comes back every 15 minutes, even while the app is closed. With Dodgeline Plus you can play as much as you like.

Dodgeline Plus

Dodgeline Plus is a one-time purchase that gives you unlimited plays and unlocks extra color themes. There is no subscription and there are no ads.

Restoring a purchase: open Dodgeline, tap the gear icon, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Other questions

No sound? Dodgeline follows your iPhone's silent switch. You can also turn sound and haptics on or off in Settings inside the game.

Worldwide leaderboard: sign in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings app, then tap the trophy in Dodgeline. Your best scores are posted automatically.

Lost my best score? Your local best is stored only on your device, so deleting the app resets it. Scores already posted to the Game Center leaderboard stay there.
